## Project Description

University clubs and student organizations rely on many different tools like spreadsheets, group chats, etc just to plan events, track budgets, and coordinate members. This can lead to miscommunication, budget mismanagement, missed deadlines, and duplicated effort.

The **Club Events Management System** addresses this need by providing a centralized web-based platform where student clubs can manage their events, finances, and internal communication in one place.

**Intended Users:**
- **Club Presidents/Leaders** - create and manage events, oversee budgets, and assign tasks to members.
- **Club Members** — view upcoming events, RSVP or sign up for tasks, and communicate with their team.
- **University Advisors** - monitor club activity and approve budgets or events where required.

**Main Features:**
- Event creation and scheduling with details such as date, location, and description.
- Budget tracking and expense management for club funds.
- Team communication tools (announcements, messaging, or discussion boards).
- Member and role management within each club.

**Key Functionalities:**
- Create, edit, and cancel club events, with visibility to relevant members.
- Track budget allocation, expenses, and remaining funds per event or club.
- Send notifications/announcements to club members.
- Manage member roles and permissions (e.g., officer vs. general member).
- Provide a dashboard summarizing upcoming events and current budget status.

# AI Usage Declaration — Lab 6

In accordance with the AUS academic integrity code and this lab's requirement to declare and justify any use of AI tools, this is a declaration of AI assistance used in preparing this (group) Lab 6 submission.

**Tool used:** Claude (Anthropic), used as an AI coding/diagramming assistant.

**How it was used:**
- Generated the UML Sequence Diagrams (`.drawio` files) for the three selected use cases (UC11 – Create Event Proposal, UC07 – Review Budget Request, UC02 – View Dashboard), including lifelines, activation bars, messages, combined fragments (`alt`, `loop`, `opt`), and notes, based on the team's own, previously completed Lab 3 use cases and Lab 5 class diagram.
- Cross-checked every sequence-diagram message against the team's Lab 5 class/method signatures to keep the two models consistent, and drafted the Exercise 1 selected-use-case table and the Consistency Check write-up (Checks A/B/C) summarized in this submission.
- Assisted with syncing and committing the deliverables to the team's shared GitHub repository.

**What was not AI-generated:** The underlying use cases (Lab 3) and class diagram/attributes/operations (Lab 5) that these sequence diagrams are built from are the team's own prior work, not generated as part of this declaration.

**Justification:** The team used AI assistance to accelerate the mechanical/diagramming portion of the Behavioral Modeling exercise and to help verify consistency against the already-completed Lab 3 and Lab 5 artifacts, consistent with this being a group deliverable with no individual-attribution requirement. All content was reviewed by the team before submission.

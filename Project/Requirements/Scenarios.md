Ahmed Abdelmohsen Accounts, Roles, Notifications, and Dashboard


Ahmed is an Executive in a student club at AUS. He logs into the Student Club Event Approval and Budget Request System using his account. After logging in, the system recognizes his role and displays his dashboard

From the dashboard, Ahmed can see the club's upcoming events, submitted event requests, approval statuses, and recent notifications. He notices a notification that one of the club's events has been approved. He opens the notification and views the updated event information.

The dashboard only shows Ahmed the features and information that he is allowed to access based on his role in the club.



\## Abdelrahman Ahmed Elsayed — Budget Requests



Scenario 1: Abdelrahman is the Treasurer of his club. After his club's event request for a movie night gets approved by the President, he logs into the Student Club Event Approval and Budget Request System to request a budget for it. He fills in the estimated costs — venue booking, snacks, and equipment rental — and submits the budget request tied to that event. He can see the request sitting under "Pending" on his dashboard, and a day later gets a notification that the President approved it, with the amount now deducted from the club's remaining budget.



Scenario 2: Later in the semester, Abdelrahman wants to check how much of the club's total budget has already been spent before submitting another request. He opens the budget summary section, which shows the total allocated club budget, the amount spent on already-approved events, and what's left. Seeing the remaining balance is lower than expected, he decides to request a smaller amount for the next event and adjusts his numbers before submitting.



## Haziq Khalid — Event Creation, Cancellation, and Approval



Scenario 1: Haziq is the President of the Robotics Club and wants to run an Arduino workshop on October 26 from 5 to 7 PM. Haziq logs in, fills in the event form with the title, a short description, the date and time, room EB2-130, and an expected turnout of 40 students, then hits submit. The system warns that the Chess Club already has an approved event in EB2-130 at 6 PM that day, so Haziq changes the room to EB1-201 and submits again. The workshop shows as "Pending" until Dr. Khan, the faculty member in charge of allocating rooms in EB1, approves it the next day. A week before the workshop, the guest instructor drops out. Haziq opens the event, clicks Cancel, and types "Guest instructor no longer available" as the reason. The workshop stays in the club's event list marked "Cancelled" instead of disappearing.



Scenario 2: Dr. Saleh is the faculty advisor for the Robotics Club and the Photography Club. Any trip outside the university needs a faculty member to go with the students, so off-campus trip proposals from these two clubs go to Dr. Saleh. On Monday morning, Dr. Saleh logs in and opens the list of pending trips, with the nearest dates at the top. The first one, a Photography Club photo walk at the zoo on October 10, fits Dr. Saleh's schedule, so it gets approved and Dr. Saleh is listed as the faculty member going on the trip. The second is a Robotics Club factory tour on November 3, but Dr. Saleh is away at a conference that week, so the trip gets sent back with a comment asking for a different date. The third is a Robotics Club trip on December 14, which is during finals week, so Dr. Saleh rejects it and writes "Club events are not allowed during finals" as the reason. Later that day, Haziq sees the factory tour marked "Changes Requested" with the comment attached, moves it to November 12, and resubmits it.
